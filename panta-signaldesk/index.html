Methodology

Every score has a reason

SignalDesk is an intelligence layer, not a black box.

01

Conviction

How far the current YES probability is from 50%. This measures decisiveness, not correctness.

02

Attention

Relative activity derived from returned volume, liquidity and trade/activity fields across the currently loaded market set.

03

Data quality

Coverage of probability, volume, liquidity, activity, status and resolution fields. Missing fields explicitly reduce confidence.

04

Risk flags

Highlights extreme probabilities, thin liquidity, missing resolution context and incomplete source data.
