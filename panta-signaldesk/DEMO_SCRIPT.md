# SignalDesk Video Scripts

## 2–3 minute pitch video

**0:00–0:20 — Hook / problem**  
Prediction markets are becoming a real-time information layer, but most people still consume them as long lists of isolated markets. The signal is there — the workflow is not.

**0:20–0:45 — Product**  
SignalDesk is an intelligence cockpit built on Panta. It turns live Panta markets into an explainable view of probability, attention, conviction and data quality, so users can see where market belief is strongest and where activity is clustering.

**0:45–1:30 — Demo**  
Open the live dashboard. Show API Health as Live and the current market count. Search or filter a market. Open a market card and show the Signal Brief. Explain that Conviction is distance from 50%, Attention is relative activity from live Panta fields, and Data Quality falls when source fields are missing. Add the market to the local watchlist and show related-market comparison. If the connected environment supports it, show read-only wallet position lookup. Finish by opening the source market on Panta.

**1:30–2:00 — Why Panta**  
Panta is the core infrastructure, not a badge. SignalDesk uses its market discovery and live market data directly, and keeps the API key server-side. We intentionally do not fabricate fallback markets — if a field is unavailable, the product says so.

**2:00–2:30 — Market / business**  
The first users are traders, researchers, creators and AI products that need a clean prediction-market intelligence layer. The distribution wedge is shareable signals and embedded intelligence, with pro alerts, team workspaces and API access as paid products.

**2:30–2:45 — Close**  
SignalDesk turns prediction markets from a destination into an information primitive. Built on Panta for Crypto World’s Fair.

## Product demo video — under 3 minutes

1. Load `https://panta-signaldesk-production.vercel.app/`.
2. Point out `API health: Live`, market count, categories, and last sync.
3. Click **Refresh live data** and show the sync timestamp change.
4. Use search/category/sort controls.
5. Select a market.
6. Show YES probability, Conviction, Attention, Data Quality, interpretation and risk flags.
7. Save the market to the watchlist.
8. Show Signal Radar and related-market comparison.
9. Optionally test a read-only wallet address if the Panta environment supports positions.
10. Click **Open on Panta** to demonstrate execution handoff.
11. Close on the methodology section and the statement that no fallback markets are fabricated.
